# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is **Risum Cyprus Limited**, registration number HE 357486, operating the **Pilates Space** studio at 58 Kolonakiou Str., 1st floor, Agios Athanasios, Limassol, Cyprus (the “Studio”, “we”, “us”).

For any questions about your data, write to us at **privacy@pilatesspace.cy**. Requests concerning personal data can also be handed to an administrator at the Studio’s front desk. We respond within one month.

## 2. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how we process the data of the Studio’s clients and the users of our app (mobile and web), including parents managing children’s accounts, guests invited by our clients, and people who have registered but not yet started training. You acknowledge this policy when registering in the app; guests are shown it when signing documents via their personal link.

## 3. What data we collect

We collect only what the Studio needs to operate:

- **profile data** — first name, last name, phone number; optionally — date of birth and email;
- **training data** — bookings, attendance, packages and credit balance, purchase history;
- **payment data** — the amount, method and status of payments. Card payments are processed by certified payment processors (for example, Viva.com and JCC); we never receive your full card number or security code. We keep the processor’s payment confirmation, which contains general details about the payer and the card used;
- **health data** — only what you have told us yourself in your health note (Section 5);
- **signed documents** — which versions of our documents you signed, when, from which device and IP address; this is our — and your — legal record;
- **technical data** — your login phone number (verification codes are handled by the authentication provider and are not stored by us), device tokens for push notifications, a log of notifications sent to you;
- **service notes** — administrators’ working notes related to serving you.

If a client invites you as a guest, they provide your first and last name, your date of birth (this is how the system tells an adult guest from a child), your phone number and, optionally, your email — so that we can send you the documents to sign and book you into a class. From then on your data is processed in the same way as a client’s.

Biometrics (Face ID / fingerprint), if you enable biometric login, stay on your device — we never receive or store them.

## 4. Why we process data, and on what legal bases

- **to provide our services** — your account, bookings, packages, payments, service notifications (confirmations, reminders, schedule changes) — basis: performance of our contract with you;
- **to run classes safely** — processing of health data — basis: your explicit consent (Section 5);
- **to comply with the law** — accounting and tax records, retention of financial documents — basis: legal obligation;
- **to protect the Studio and its clients** — the log of signed documents, change logs, abuse prevention, defence against potential claims — basis: our legitimate interest;
- **to send news and offers** — only if you have separately opted in (Section 12) — basis: your consent.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling with legal effects on you.

## 5. Health data

Health information is a special category of personal data (Article 9 GDPR). We process it only on the basis of your explicit consent, given when you sign the Informed Consent, and for one purpose only — running your classes safely.

- health data is stored encrypted; every change is recorded in an audit log;
- **your instructor sees your health note only in connection with the classes you are booked into with them — only for a limited time before and after each class, and on a read-only basis.** This is what lets the instructor know about your limitations and run your session safely;
- only the Studio’s administration has standing access;
- health data is never used for marketing and never shared with third parties;
- you can delete your health note at any time in your profile settings — we stop processing this data; the note’s previous text is kept, encrypted, in the change log and is erased when your data is anonymised (Section 10).

## 6. Who sees your data within the Studio

Access to data follows the principle of minimum necessity: administrators see the data needed to serve clients; instructors see the client cards of their own classes (contact details, package balances and upcoming bookings) and, for a limited time, their health notes (Section 5); the owner is responsible for the system as a whole. Changes to sensitive data are logged.

## 7. Service providers who help us operate

We use processor companies. They process data on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication and storage for the app; data is hosted in the EU;
- **Viva.com** — online payment processing (a licensed payment provider; you enter card details on their side);
- **JCC** — processing card payments at the Studio’s terminal;
- **Twilio** — sending SMS verification codes and SMS notifications;
- **Resend** — sending email notifications; data is processed in the EU;
- **Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (APNs)** — delivering push notifications to your device;
- infrastructure providers hosting the app’s backend — a current list is available on request.

## 8. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (for example Twilio, Google, Apple) may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases the transfer relies on European Commission adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

## 9. How long we keep data

- **profile data and training history** — for as long as you remain a client; after an erasure request — anonymisation (Section 10);
- **financial records and issued invoices** — for the period required by Cyprus tax and company law (at least six years from the end of the relevant tax year), even if the account has been deleted;
- **health data** — for as long as your note exists; its previous versions stay, encrypted, in the change log until anonymisation;
- **the log of signed documents and document versions** — kept without a time limit: they evidence which terms applied between us and protect both sides in a dispute;
- **the notification log** — 12 months;
- **data of people who registered but never started training** — for as long as the registration remains relevant; at least once a year the Studio reviews long-inactive registrations and anonymises them.

## 10. Erasure and anonymisation

You can request erasure of your data at any time — by writing to **privacy@pilatesspace.cy**, via the Studio’s messenger, or at the front desk. The request is carried out by the Studio; we may ask you to confirm your identity.

We anonymise your data: your name, phone number, date of birth and email are replaced with de-identified values, your health note is erased, and your login account is deleted entirely. Financial records that we are legally required to keep are retained — including invoices already issued, together with the buyer name and details stated on them: an issued fiscal document is immutable, and keeping it is our legal obligation. The documents you signed are also retained as evidence that they were signed. In our internal records you appear, after anonymisation, as a de-identified client. An anonymised account cannot be restored: if you come back, we start from a clean slate.

## 11. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

- access your data and obtain a copy in a machine-readable format — we prepare the copy on request;
- correct inaccurate data — most of it can be edited directly in your profile, the rest through an administrator;
- request erasure of your data (Section 10);
- restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- withdraw your marketing consent in the app settings — withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. You can stop the processing of your health data by deleting your note (Section 5): the signed document remains in force, and you can add a new note without signing again;
- receive, on request, a copy of the exact version of a document that you signed;
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Cyprus this is the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, www.dataprotection.gov.cy.

To exercise your rights, write to us or speak to an administrator — the app has no dedicated buttons for these requests; they are handled manually and free of charge.

## 12. Marketing messages

We send Studio news, promotions and offers only with your separate consent — its terms are set out in the short “Marketing Communications Consent” document, the checkbox is unticked by default, and the channels (email, SMS, push) are named directly in it. You can opt out at any time in your profile settings, and we will stop immediately. Service messages (booking confirmations, reminders, schedule changes) do not depend on marketing consent; you can choose their channels in the app’s notification settings.

## 13. Minors

The account of a client under 18 is managed by a parent or legal guardian: they sign the documents, pay for the child’s classes — from their own packages in the app, or by buying a package for the child at the front desk — and provide the child’s health information. Under Cyprus law the age of independent digital consent is 14; in our app, everything concerning a client under 18 is in any case handled by the parent.

## 14. How we protect data

Data travels over secure channels and is stored with providers meeting modern security standards. Particularly sensitive data (health notes) is additionally encrypted at rest. Staff access is role-restricted, and changes to sensitive data are logged. Backups are also stored encrypted.

## 15. Data from our previous system

Before launching the app, the Studio kept client records in a third-party CRM system. On migration we transferred into our own system clients’ first and last names and phone numbers, class balances and upcoming group bookings — this was necessary to continue providing our services; attendance and payment history, emails and dates of birth were not transferred. On completion of the migration we are securing the deletion of our data by the former provider. On first login to the app we ask you to accept the Terms & Conditions and this Policy; the Liability Waiver and the Informed Consent are signed before your first class booking.

## 16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. For material changes (new processing purposes, new data recipients, changed retention periods) we will ask you to accept the new version in the app and tell you about the changes. Minor edits are published without re-acceptance. All previous versions are retained.
