You log in to the app with your phone number and an SMS code; if the code does not reach you, the Studio can send you a one-time login link. ## 3. Classes and schedule

The Studio offers group classes on equipment, private and duet sessions, and stretching; the Studio may also hold workshops, seminars and other events. The current schedule, class duration and prices are always shown in the app. Where special conditions apply to a particular format, promo materials or an administrator will tell you about them.

## 4. Booking

Classes are booked through the app and paid for in advance — with package credits or a one-off payment. Your place in the group is reserved from the moment your booking is confirmed. For each class format the Studio sets a deadline until which cancellation is free; the app shows the exact time in your booking confirmation and in booking management.

Besides yourself, you can book classes for people linked to your account: a partner, a child or an adult guest. The person who makes the booking pays for it; the app shows which packages are available at the moment of booking. Before their first class, a guest signs the Liability Waiver and the Informed Consent themselves — via a personal link from the app.

## 5. Packages and credits

A package is a set of class credits. Every package has a validity period; the app shows the package’s contents, price and validity at the time of purchase — please check them before paying. When a package expires, unused credits are forfeited.

- in exceptional cases an administrator may extend a package’s validity period;
- some packages can be shared with people linked to your account (for example, a partner or child) — shared packages can be used for bookings by both of you;
- promotional offers (such as packages for new clients) run on their own conditions — promo materials or an administrator will tell you about them;
- credits are personal; transferring a remaining balance to another person is possible in exceptional cases at the Studio’s discretion — speak to an administrator.

## 6. Cancellations, no-shows and late arrival

Plans change — you can cancel bookings in the app. Each class has a cancellation window. If you cancel within the window, the credit returns to the package the booking was paid from. If you cancel later, the credit is deducted, and the deduction is final: it does not depend on whether someone else takes the freed-up spot. This is how we protect the group and the other clients; a freed-up spot may be offered to the waitlist.

Failing to attend a class without cancelling the booking counts as a late cancellation — the credit is deducted.

Please arrive a few minutes early: the warm-up is part of a safe workout. If you are more than 10 minutes late, we will unfortunately not be able to admit you to the class — starting without a warm-up is unsafe, and joining mid-class disturbs the group. In that case the credit is deducted, as with a no-show.

## 7. Cancellation by the Studio

Sometimes we have to cancel a class — for example, if too few participants have signed up, an instructor falls ill, or something unforeseen happens. In that case we will notify you through the app, and your credit will automatically return to the package the booking was paid from — you can use it for another class.

## 8. Prices and payment

All classes and packages are paid for in advance, through the app or at the Studio’s front desk, unless the Studio has expressly agreed otherwise with you; payments are handled by a licensed payment provider. Current prices are shown in the app. We may change prices and the schedule; we will give advance notice of price changes, and changes do not affect packages you have already purchased.

## 9. Refunds

A purchase in the app is a distance purchase, so you have the right to withdraw from it within **14 days** of purchase without giving a reason — provided the package has not been fully used. On withdrawal we will refund the unused part: the value of the classes you have attended, calculated pro rata to the package price, is deducted from the refund.

After 14 days from purchase, payments for packages and classes are non-refundable; credits can be used until the package expires. If you are unable to attend for a longer period — speak to an administrator: in exceptional cases the Studio may extend your package’s validity.

If the Studio cancels paid classes and cannot offer a replacement, or ceases to provide its services, the unused balance is refunded in money.

Nothing in this section limits the rights granted to you by consumer protection law.

## 10. Studio rules

A few simple rules that keep classes comfortable for everyone:

- arrive a few minutes before class — you will have time to change and start the warm-up calmly;
- train in comfortable sportswear; socks are required in class; please leave street shoes outside the workout area;
- switch your phone to silent — protect the atmosphere of the class;
- after class, wipe down the reformer, return the springs to their starting position and hang the straps back in place;
- be respectful of other clients and the Studio team.
