# Liability Waiver

## 1. Who we are and what this document is

This document is an agreement between you and **Risum Cyprus Limited**, registration number HE 357486, the company operating the **Pilates Space** studio at 58 Kolonakiou Str., 1st floor, Agios Athanasios, Limassol, Cyprus. Below we simply call the company the “Studio”, “we” or “us”.

Pilates is physical exercise, and all physical exercise carries certain risks. This document describes those risks honestly and records that you accept them knowingly and voluntarily. We ask you to sign it once — in the app, before your first class booking.

## 2. What this document covers

This document applies to all of the Studio’s services and to your entire time on the Studio’s premises:

- group classes on equipment (Reformer and other studio apparatus);
- private (one-to-one) and duet sessions;
- stretching, workshops, seminars and other Studio classes and events;
- your presence on the Studio’s premises: the class rooms, changing rooms and reception area.

## 3. The nature of our classes

Our classes are held on Reformer machines — apparatus with a moving carriage, springs of varying resistance, straps and handles — as well as with other Pilates equipment. Classes involve physical effort and include stretching, strength and balance exercises. Intensity depends on the class format and is managed by the instructor.

## 4. Risks

Even with correct technique, an attentive instructor and well-maintained equipment, physical exercise carries inherent risks that cannot be eliminated entirely. These include, among others:

- muscle and ligament strains, bruises;
- aggravation of pre-existing injuries or medical conditions;
- dizziness, nausea, brief loss of consciousness;
- in rare cases — more serious injuries (such as fractures or dislocations) and other health consequences.

By signing this document, you confirm that you understand these risks and accept them voluntarily.

## 5. What we do on our side

We take safety seriously:

- classes are led by experienced instructors with professional Pilates training;
- before you start training, you receive an introduction to the equipment;
- we keep the equipment in good working order;
- the instructor takes into account the health information you provided when signing the Informed Consent.

## 6. What we ask of you

- follow the instructor’s directions and use the equipment only as shown;
- disclose your health conditions honestly when signing the Informed Consent, and update that information if anything changes;
- if you feel pain, dizziness or significant discomfort during a class — stop immediately and tell the instructor;
- do not attend classes under the influence of alcohol or substances affecting coordination or attention;
- observe the Studio’s rules set out in the Terms & Conditions.

## 7. Release of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by the law of the Republic of Cyprus, you waive any claims against the Studio, its directors, employees and instructors in respect of personal injury or other loss arising from the inherent risks of exercise described in Section 4 — that is, risks which remain even when we exercise due care.

Nothing in this document excludes or limits the Studio’s liability for harm caused by our negligence or intentional acts, or any other liability which cannot be excluded or limited under applicable law.

## 8. Minors

If the client is under 18, this document is signed on their behalf by a parent or legal guardian — in the parent’s own app. By signing for a child, you confirm that:

- you are the child’s parent or legal guardian and are entitled to make such decisions on their behalf;
- you have read the risks described above and accept them on the child’s behalf;
- you will inform the Studio about the child’s health conditions and limitations (via the Informed Consent);
- you will explain to the child that they must listen to the instructor and follow the rules during class.

We admit teenagers to classes from the age of 14; younger children — only in exceptional cases, in individual sessions, by agreement with the Studio. Admission to specific class formats is at the Studio’s discretion.

## 9. Personal belongings

Please do not leave valuables unattended. The Studio is not responsible for loss of or damage to personal belongings, except where caused by our negligence.

## 10. How this document is signed and how long it remains in force

This document is signed electronically — in the Studio’s app through your personal account or, in specific cases (for example, for guests of the Studio), via a personal link. In both cases we record the time of signature, the exact version of the text and who signed. This constitutes a simple electronic signature within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS).

Once signed, the document applies to all your future classes. If we materially change its terms, we will publish a new version, ask you to sign it again and tell you about the changes.

This document is governed by the law of the Republic of Cyprus. Disputes that cannot be resolved by negotiation are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the Republic of Cyprus.

This document is drawn up in Russian and English. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the English version prevails.
