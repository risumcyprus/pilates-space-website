# Marketing Communications Consent

This document is about Studio news and offers — and nothing else. By ticking the box “**I would like to receive Pilates Space news, promotions and offers — by email, SMS and push notifications**”, you give **Risum Cyprus Limited** (the **Pilates Space** studio, Limassol) your consent to send you such messages.

## 1. What we send, and how

Studio news, promotions, special offers and event invitations. Channels: email, SMS and app push notifications. We aim to write rarely and only when it matters.

## 2. Entirely optional

The box is unticked by default, and its state affects nothing else: classes, bookings and every feature of the app work the same with or without it. Service messages — booking confirmations, class reminders, schedule changes — do not depend on this consent; you can choose their channels in the app’s notification settings.

## 3. Withdraw at any time

You can switch marketing messages off in your profile settings — we stop immediately. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of messages sent before it.

## 4. Data and legal basis

To send these messages we use your name and the contact details in your profile. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules). For full details of how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

This document is drawn up in Russian and English. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the English version prevails.
