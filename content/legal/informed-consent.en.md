# Informed Consent (Health & Exercise)

## 1. What this document is

This document is about your health and your informed decision to exercise. It complements the Liability Waiver and is signed together with it in the app, before your first class booking. It does three things: you tell us about your health, you confirm that your decision to exercise is an informed one, and you give consent to the processing of your health data.

The party to this agreement is **Risum Cyprus Limited**, registration number HE 357486, operating the **Pilates Space** studio at 58 Kolonakiou Str., 1st floor, Agios Athanasios, Limassol, Cyprus — the “Studio”, “we” or “us”.

## 2. Why we ask about your health

Equipment-based Pilates suits almost any fitness level, but some conditions require modified exercises, reduced load, or a conversation with a doctor first. Your instructor can only make a class safe for you if they know about your limitations.

## 3. What you should tell us about

Please let us know if you have, or have recently had:

- heart or vascular conditions, high or low blood pressure;
- injuries or surgery, especially within the last 12 months;
- spine or joint problems, hernias;
- osteoporosis or reduced bone density;
- pregnancy (at any stage) or recent childbirth;
- episodes of dizziness or fainting;
- chronic conditions affecting your tolerance of physical exercise;
- any restrictions or recommendations given to you by a doctor.

This list is a guide, not an exhaustive catalogue. If you are unsure whether something is worth mentioning — mention it: there is no such thing as too much of this information.

## 4. Your declaration

When you sign, the app will ask you to tick two mandatory statements:

- “**I have no medical contraindications to exercise, or I will disclose them to the studio before class**”.
- “**I understand the risks of exercise**”.

The first statement is not a claim that you are perfectly healthy. It means: either you know of no contraindications, or you will disclose them to the Studio before class — in the health information field, to an administrator, or to your instructor. The second refers to the inherent risks of exercise described in the Liability Waiver.

Next to these you will find an optional free-text field for injuries, medical conditions and pregnancy. We strongly encourage you to fill it in if there is anything to tell: it directly shapes how your instructor will run your class.

Important: your health information is a self-declaration. We do not carry out medical examinations and we rely on what you tell us. The completeness and accuracy of this information is your responsibility (for a child, the parent’s responsibility).

## 5. Pregnancy

Pregnancy is not a bar to Pilates, but it does require a special approach. If you are pregnant or have recently given birth, you must tell us before booking a class. We do not start training new clients during pregnancy. For clients already training at the Studio, continuing to train during pregnancy is possible at the Studio’s individual discretion — as a rule, where you have a history of regular training at the Studio, and only provided that you have consulted your doctor and your doctor has no objection to you exercising. We will ask you to confirm that consultation with an entry in your health profile. The Studio may restrict admission to classes at certain stages of pregnancy or offer a private format instead of group classes. An administrator will tell you the current admission rules by stage of pregnancy; the decision to train during pregnancy is one you take together with your doctor.

## 6. The Studio’s right to request medical clearance

If the information you provide suggests that exercise may be unsafe for you, we may ask you to provide a doctor’s clearance for exercise and, until then, may cancel your bookings and decline admission to classes. This is a rare measure, and it is about your safety, not formality.

## 7. Your health information in the app

What you have told us is stored in your profile as your health note. You can add to or change it at any time. Please keep it up to date whenever anything changes: a new injury, surgery, pregnancy. If the note has not been updated for a long time, the app will from time to time gently remind you after you make a booking.

## 8. Consent to the processing of health data

Health information is a special category of personal data (Article 9 GDPR), and its processing requires your explicit consent. By signing this document, you agree that if you provide the Studio with information about your health — when signing or at any later point — the Studio processes it for one purpose only: running your classes safely.

How we handle this data:

- the data is stored encrypted, and every change is recorded in an audit log;
- your instructor sees your health note only in connection with the classes you are booked into with them — only for a limited time before and after each class, and on a read-only basis. This is what allows them to run your session safely;
- only the Studio’s administration has standing access;
- we never use health data for marketing and never share it with third parties.

For full details of how we process personal data, see our Privacy Policy.

## 9. Deleting your health information

You can delete your health note at any time in your profile settings. From that moment we stop processing this data; the note’s previous text is kept, encrypted, in the change log and is erased only when your data is anonymised. The signed document — and your consent — remains in force: you can keep training and create a new note without signing again. Please note: without health information, your instructor will not be able to take your limitations into account in class.

Full erasure of your data is available by requesting account deletion; how that works is described in the Privacy Policy.

## 10. Minors

For a client under 18, this document is signed by a parent or legal guardian — in the parent’s own app. When signing for a child, the mandatory statements read: “{Child’s name} has no medical contraindications to exercise, or I will disclose them to the studio before class” and “I understand the risks of exercise for {Child’s name}”. The parent provides the child’s health information, is responsible for its completeness and accuracy, and updates it through their own app. Consent to the processing of the child’s health data is likewise given by the parent.

## 11. Signing and duration

This document is signed electronically — in the app through your personal account or, in specific cases (for example, for guests of the Studio), via a personal link. In both cases we record the time of signature, the exact version of the text and who signed.

The consent remains in force for as long as you remain a client of the Studio — or until we publish a new version with material changes, in which case we will ask you to sign again and tell you about the changes.

## 12. Governing law

This document is governed by the law of the Republic of Cyprus.

This document is drawn up in Russian and English. In the event of any discrepancy between the versions, the English version prevails.

## 13. What I confirm by signing

- I have read this document in full and understood its contents.
- I have no medical contraindications to exercise, or I will disclose them to the studio before class.
- I understand the risks of exercise.
- If I provide the Studio with information about my health — when signing or at any later point — I give my explicit consent to its processing as described in Section 8.
- If I am signing for a child — I am their parent or legal guardian, I have provided their health information, and I give this consent on their behalf.
